# Web keywords — tiered, from real Ahrefs data

**Source:** Ahrefs Keywords Explorer, pulled by the founder 2026-08-27. Volumes are Ahrefs' banded
figures (`>100K`, `>10,000`, `>1000`, `>100`); KD is Ahrefs' Easy/Medium/Hard label. Only seeds that
returned meaningful volume were kept.

---

## THE FINDING THAT REORDERS EVERYTHING

The duration terms are **enormous and Easy at the same time**. That combination is rare, and it is
sitting unclaimed:

| Keyword | Volume | KD |
|---|---|---|
| `30 minute timer` | **>100K** | **Easy** |
| `25 minute timer` | >10,000 | **Easy** |
| `45 minute timer` | >10,000 | **Easy** |
| `50 minute timer` | >10,000 | **Easy** |
| `2 hour timer` | >10,000 | **Easy** |
| `3 hour timer` | >10,000 | **Easy** |

Meanwhile every term the strategy had assumed was the target is **Hard**: `pomodoro timer` (>10,000),
`focus timer` (>1000), `study timer` (>1000), `countdown timer` (>10,000).

So the plan inverts. **Duration pages are not the long tail — they are the primary play.** Pomodoro
becomes the positioning layer we win slowly, not the term we chase first.

`hourglass timer` is Easy but only `>100`. Still worth owning — free, uncontested, and literally the
product — but it is a differentiator, not a traffic source. Right to keep in the title, wrong to
build a strategy on.

## Build order this implies

1. **Duration pages** — 25 / 30 / 45 / 50 / 90 min and 1 / 2 / 3 h. Tier 1a alone is >150K combined
   volume at Easy difficulty. Each page opens the timer **already configured**, because the searcher
   wants a running timer, not an article about one. The deep-link plumbing already exists
   (`?mode=flow&min=30`), so a page is metadata, unique content, and a pre-set start.
2. **Sand / hourglass cluster** — `/sand-timer/`, `/hourglass-timer/`. Low volume, no competition,
   and it is the honest description of the product.
3. **Guide pages** — Tier 3e and 3f. Hard but informational: they rank on depth over time and they
   feed internal links down into the duration pages.
4. **Homepage** keeps the pomodoro/hourglass positioning. It will not win `pomodoro timer` soon and
   does not need to.

## Rules the data produced

- **Easy + big beats Hard + bigger.** `30 minute timer` at >100K/Easy is worth more to this domain
  than `online timer` at >100K/Hard, which we should not touch for years.
- **Never build a page per modifier.** `set a 30 minute timer`, `30 minute timer google` and
  `30 minute timer` are one intent. One page; the variants appear naturally in the copy.
- **`far cry 6 special operations 90 minute timer`** shows up under `90 minute timer` at >100. It is a
  video-game query. Recorded so nobody optimises for it by accident.
